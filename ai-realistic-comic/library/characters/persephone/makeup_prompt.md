# Persephone / 珀耳塞福涅 — makeup sheet

Adult woman. Human ears. Two legs.

## Face 脸

Warm fair skin, soft oval face, amber-brown eyes, full rose lips, a calm half-smile. Late twenties.

暖白皮，柔和鹅蛋脸，琥珀棕眼，玫瑰唇。成年。

## Hair 发

Long wavy auburn to copper hair. Sometimes a loose side braid. Crown of dark leaves, small red berries, and wheat ears.

赤褐长卷发，深色叶、红浆果、麦穗花冠。

## Costume 衣

Off-the-shoulder dark green sheer chiffon over a dark bodice, floor length, high slit, crimson silk sash at the waist, one wide gold cuff on the upper arm, bare feet. The standing shot shows the dress more closed; the throne shot shows the slit and a bare thigh. The sheet uses the full standing costume with the slit, sash, cuff, crown, and bare feet. Human ears, not pointed.

墨绿露肩纱裙，深红腰封，宽金臂环，赤足。人耳，不是尖耳。

## Palette and light 色与光

Forest green, black, crimson, gold, auburn. Scene light is low amber candlelight. The sheet uses even grey studio light.
