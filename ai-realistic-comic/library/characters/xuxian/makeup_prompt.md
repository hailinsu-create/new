# 许仙 / Xu Xian — makeup sheet

Adult man. Human. Two legs. No tail.

## Face 脸

Fair skin, clean-shaven, straight nose, dark brown eyes, soft jaw, gentle expression. Late twenties.

白净无须，直鼻，深棕眼，下颌柔和。成年男性。

## Hair 发

Black hair in a neat topknot. Dark indigo cloth headband across the forehead.

黑发束髻，深蓝布抹额。

## Costume 衣

Dark indigo robe worn open at the chest, white inner collar, dark sash. Sleeves sometimes rolled to the forearm, as in the rain. A bamboo medicine bucket hangs from a brown strap across the torso. The square wooden box in the pavilion is the same medicine prop set on the floor. Standing sheet shows the full robe, two human legs, and simple dark cloth shoes.

靛蓝交领袍，胸口微敞，白内领，深色腰带。肩背竹药筒。全身两条人腿。

Indigo, white collar, bamboo tan. Scene light is teal rain and one warm lantern. The sheet uses even grey studio light.
