# Fanvue sign-up walkthrough (UI verified read-only on 2026-10-01; nothing was submitted)

You will need: a laptop or phone with a camera, a valid government ID or passport, 15 minutes, and a payout method.
Have the copy from `profile.md` open in another tab.

## Step 1 - Fan account
1. Go to https://www.fanvue.com and click **Sign Up** (top right).
2. Use either **Continue with Google** / **Continue with X**, or **Email + Password**.
   Use an email dedicated to this page (not your main one); a stage name is fine later. Use a password manager.
3. The page states that continuing means you accept the Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy and Content Creator Agreement and
   confirm you are at least 18. Read them; this is your legal acceptance. **Stay Updated** is an optional newsletter toggle.
4. Click **Sign Up**.

## Step 2 - Become a creator
1. In the menu click **Become a creator**.
2. Choose the type: **AI Creator** ("primarily generated or significantly edited using AI tools"). Do not pick Human Creator.
3. Fill in the profile: stage name, short bio (paste the AI-disclosure line from `profile.md`), and the content type.

## Step 3 - Verification (KYC)
1. Follow the prompts for identity verification: photo of a valid, in-date, government-issued international ID or passport, plus a
   live selfie. Allow the camera in your browser.
2. This is required even though no real face appears on the page, and it is not shown publicly.
3. If it fails: use a passport, good light, no glare. One creator account per person (AI creators may later link more).
   Details: https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/9506878-id-verification-passing-kyc

## Step 4 - Security and payouts
1. Settings > turn on **2-factor authentication**.
2. Set a payout method (bank transfer, Cosmo eWallet, MassPay or crypto; PayPal is not supported). Fanvue shows a 1% payout fee.
3. Fees: 15% platform fee for the first 30 days after KYC, then 20%. Chargeback fee $50 if disputes exceed 1.5%.
4. You can withdraw once KYC is approved, payouts are enabled, balance is about $20+, and at least 5 posts exist.

## Step 5 - Finish the page (before telling anyone)
1. Upload profile picture and banner (use the stamped previews in `output/launch/fanvue/`).
2. Paste the bio, subscription description, welcome message and pinned post from `profile.md`.
3. Set the subscription price (suggested $7.99/month; allowed $3.99-$100).
4. Upload the first posts from `content-plan.md` (Fanvue recommends about 10 pieces before promoting).
5. On every post keep the AI disclosure (caption line or the stamp; both are already prepared).
