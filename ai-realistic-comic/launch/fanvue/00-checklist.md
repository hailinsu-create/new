# Fanvue launch checklist

Everything marked **[ME]** is already prepared in this folder or in `output/launch/fanvue/` (images are not committed;
regenerate with `comic export-stills`). Everything marked **[YOU]** needs your identity, device, money or a legal acceptance
and cannot be done for you.

Facts below come from Fanvue's help centre (checked 2026-10-01); re-check before acting, rules change.
- AI policy: https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/9538738-is-ai-content-allowed-on-fanvue
- Creator account: https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/9506869-creating-a-fanvue-creator-account
- KYC: https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/9506878-id-verification-passing-kyc and https://help.fanvue.com/en/articles/9539091-passing-kyc-and-creating-multiple-accounts-as-an-ai-creator

## 1. Account (about 15 minutes)
- [YOU] fanvue.com > Sign Up with an email/password you control (a stage name and a separate email are fine; real identity is only
  needed to get paid and is not shown publicly).
- [YOU] "Become a creator", choose **AI creator**, finish the profile.
- [YOU] KYC: valid government ID or passport + live selfie (camera permission). Needed even though no real face appears.
- [YOU] Turn on 2-factor authentication. Set up a payout method (bank transfer, Cosmo eWallet, MassPay or crypto; PayPal is
  not supported). Minimum withdrawal is about $20; at least 5 posts are required before the first payout.
- [ME] Fanvue's own rule: the account owner must be 18+; AI media must not depict a real person other than the owner and must not
  resemble anyone under 18. All characters here are fictional adults (see `manifest.json`: declared age look per character).

## 2. Profile
- [ME] Stage-name ideas, bio (zh/en), AI-disclosure line, welcome message, pinned post, FAQ replies: `profile.md`.
- [YOU] Pick the name, paste the copy, upload a profile picture and banner. Suggested: crop the faces from
  `myth-hades-persephone__throne-pomegranate_preview.jpg` and `legend-white-snake__coil-embrace_preview.jpg` (already
  stamped AI-Generated).
- [YOU] Make sure the AI tag/disclosure appears in the bio (Fanvue also adds an AI tag to AI creator accounts).

## 3. Content
- [ME] 8 finished stills, each as a full-size file and a 1080px free preview, both stamped "AI-Generated", with
  `manifest.json` (model, prompt hash, score, declared ages, spend): `output/launch/fanvue/`.
- [ME] 14-day posting plan with a caption for every image (zh/en): `content-plan.md`.
- [YOU] Look at every image yourself before posting (hands, feet, ears). Automated anatomy checks have missed real defects.
- [YOU] Upload in the order of the plan; Fanvue recommends about 10 pieces and automated messages before promoting.
  The plan counts 8 stills + 8 teasers; generate 2-4 more stills if you want more depth (about $0.37 each).

## 4. Pricing (decide yourself)
- Subscriptions can be set from $3.99 to $100 per month; paid posts $3-$500; Fanvue keeps 15% for 30 days after KYC, then 20%.
- Suggested start: $7.99/month with the previews free. Raise after you have 20+ pieces.

## 5. Before every post
- Caption or watermark must say the media is AI-generated (the stamp already does; the captions repeat it).
- Nothing explicit: fal's policy and the platform's moderation both apply; all images here are clothed and suggestive.
- No real-person names or likenesses; the characters are original designs inspired by public-domain myth and legend.
