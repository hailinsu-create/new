# Pangchuang terms of use

Last updated: 2026-09-01

Using Pangchuang means you agree to these terms and the privacy policy.

## Service

- Demo mode is free. It does not capture the real screen or call a vision API.
- Full companion is a Google Play one-time product (`full_unlock`, about $0.99).
- You configure and pay for your own vision API.

## Purchase

Payments and refunds follow Google Play policy. We do not collect card numbers. Restore purchases after a device change.

## Your responsibility

- Only capture screens you are allowed to capture.
- Do not use Pangchuang to spy on someone else's device.
